Phase 20A — eBay product detail hardening and Combay product image background processing

Implemented:
- Adds Combay branded product image background asset at /images/product-backgrounds/combay-background.jpg.
- Adds ProductImage metadata fields so original eBay URLs can be preserved while processed Combay-background images are used publicly.
- eBay sync now prepares imported images with originalUrl metadata and can automatically process images when enabled.
- Adds optional remove.bg-based background removal + Combay background replacement pipeline.
- Adds /api/admin/product-images/process-backgrounds to batch-process existing eBay-imported product images.
- Adds Admin > eBay button: Process image backgrounds.
- Keeps product image processing disabled by default unless env vars are configured.
- Adds .env.example entries for EBAY_IMAGE_BACKGROUND_PROCESSING_ENABLED, EBAY_IMAGE_BACKGROUND_MAX_PER_PRODUCT, REMOVE_BG_API_KEY and PRODUCT_IMAGE_BACKGROUND_URL.
- No Canva integration is required for this first implementation; remove.bg is the first supported provider.

Required Prisma push:
- ProductImage adds originalUrl, backgroundProcessedAt, backgroundProcessingStatus and backgroundProcessingError.

Required env vars to activate image processing:
EBAY_IMAGE_BACKGROUND_PROCESSING_ENABLED=true
EBAY_IMAGE_BACKGROUND_MAX_PER_PRODUCT=1
REMOVE_BG_API_KEY=...
UPLOAD_PROVIDER=vps
UPLOAD_RECEIVER_URL=...
UPLOAD_RECEIVER_SECRET=...
NEXTAUTH_URL=https://combay-website-lt8v.vercel.app

Optional:
PRODUCT_IMAGE_BACKGROUND_URL=https://your-domain/images/product-backgrounds/combay-background.jpg
