Phase 16C — Visual CMS foundation

Adds /admin/content/visual with a live public-site preview iframe, page selector, drag-and-drop ordering for CMS cards and process steps, image upload/URL editing for visual blocks, and common addable content blocks such as image cards, icon features, promotion banners, slider items and animation placeholders.

This phase intentionally keeps checkout, account, navigation and protected workflows outside visual dragging to avoid accidental site breakage.
