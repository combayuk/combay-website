Phase 15E — Marketing consent, unsubscribe links and preference controls

Built on top of deployed Phase 15D.

Adds:
- DB-backed customer marketing preferences in the customer portal.
- Separate consent switches for all marketing, new stock, promotions, monthly campaigns, seasonal campaigns and order follow-up emails.
- Category interest preferences saved to MarketingPrefs.
- One-click unsubscribe link for marketing automation emails.
- Public /unsubscribe page for customers who click email footer links.
- /api/marketing/unsubscribe endpoint for token lookup and unsubscribe updates.
- /api/account/marketing-preferences endpoint for portal preference load/save.
- Marketing automation consent checks before scheduled/monthly/seasonal campaign sends.
- Transactional emails such as signup/order/account emails remain allowed even when marketing is disabled.
- EmailAutomationLog now stores subject, preview, category, status, skipped reason and provider metadata.
- Admin automation API now reports customer/opt-in/unsubscribe/log summary stats.
- Marketing emails include List-Unsubscribe header and visible footer preference link.

Requires Prisma db push because MarketingPrefs and EmailAutomationLog were expanded.
