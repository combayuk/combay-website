Phase 13C.5 - Product image click zoom behaviour

This cumulative patch builds on Phase 13C.4.

Changes:
- Removes button-based zoom controls from the product image area.
- Main product image click cycle is now: 100% -> 125% -> 150% -> 100%.
- When zoomed at 125% or 150%, moving the cursor within the image area pans the zoomed image by updating transform-origin.
- Gallery image changes reset zoom back to 100%.
- No 360/auto-cycle feature is present.

No Prisma schema changes are required.
